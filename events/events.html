Events We Participate In

ROBOTICS COMPETITION

ENSIT ROBOT

ENSIT ROBOTS is a robotics competition that brings together passionate students to showcase their technical skills, creativity, and innovation. Participants design, build, and program robots to take on different challenges, putting their engineering knowledge and problem-solving abilities to the test. The event offers an exciting opportunity to discover innovative robotic projects, exchange ideas, and experience the spirit of teamwork and competition. ⚙️🚀.

ROBOTICS COMPETITION

ROBOTS LEAGUE

Robots League is a national robotics competition organized by IEEE RAS ESSTHS, bringing together students from across Tunisia to design, build, and compete with their robots. The event celebrates innovation, creativity, and teamwork while inspiring the next generation of robotics enthusiasts. 🤖⚙️
